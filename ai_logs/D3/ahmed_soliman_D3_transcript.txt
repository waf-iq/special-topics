  What happens if a model fails — at three levels

❯ before choosing what is the pool size ?                                       

⏺ Good thing to pin down before choosing — it's central to the trade-off. Here's
  exactly what "pool size" means in your pipeline.

  Pool size = how many candidates the retriever shortlists for the reranker

  The pipeline is two stages:

  Stage 1: retriever.search(query, POOL)   →  POOL candidate chunks   (cheap,
  BM25+dense fusion)
  Stage 2: rerank(query, those POOL, ..., top_n=5)  →  best 5
  (expensive, cross-encoder)

  So pool size is the POOL number — how deep the first stage digs before the
  reranker reorders. In the harness it's the pool argument in make_fn
  (scripts/eval_rerank.py): retriever.search(query, pool).

  - pool = 20 → retriever fetches its top 20, the reranker scores all 20
  query↔chunk pairs, returns the best 5.
  - pool = 50 → retriever fetches 50, reranker scores 50 pairs, returns the
  best 5.

  In production this is the executor's candidate_k (graphrag.py:100, default 20)
  — the executor pulls a pool, then calls your rerank(..., top_n=k).

  Why it's a knob worth tuning

  It's a quality-vs-latency dial within reranking:

  - Bigger pool = more chances to find the right chunk. If the truly-relevant
  chunk sits at retrieval rank #18, a pool of 10 never shows it to the reranker
  (so the reranker can't possibly surface it); a pool of 20 does.
  - But bigger pool = more pairs to score = more latency. The cross-encoder runs
  one transformer pass per candidate, so latency scales roughly linearly with
  pool size — visible in your sweep: bge went 615ms → 1145ms → 1925ms → 3129ms
  as pool went 10 → 20 → 30 → 50.
  - And too big can hurt quality — more distractors give the reranker more ways
  to make a mistake. That's why both bge and minilm peaked at pool 20 and
  dropped at 30/50.

  Why "pool 20" is in the recommendation

  Your sweep found pool = 20 is the sweet spot for both cross-encoders — best
  NDCG@5 and lower latency than 30/50:

  ┌───────────────┬─────────┬─────────┬─────────┬─────────┐
  │   reranker    │ pool 10 │ pool 20 │ pool 30 │ pool 50 │
  ├───────────────┼─────────┼─────────┼─────────┼─────────┤
  │ minilm NDCG@5 │ 0.563   │ 0.608   │ 0.601   │ 0.601   │

  └───────────────┴─────────┴─────────┴─────────┴─────────┘

  executor's candidate_k=20 (that's the integrator's graphrag.py/api.py, so a
