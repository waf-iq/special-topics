  - Corpus: SciFact (5,183 abstracts) + 5 arXiv demo PDFs, embedded with
  BGE-small-en-v1.5 (384-dim)
  - AutoML: Optuna HPO with 5 methods (Grid, Random, TPE, Hyperband, BOHB) — all
   converge to the same config neighborhood. BOHB chosen as blessed method.
  - Online Learning: River-based contextual bandit with 3 learner variants,
  ADWIN drift detection, 2000-event stream with 2 drift points
  - MLflow: Experiment tracking across all 5 HPO methods
  - Key outputs: configs/winning_runcard.yaml, reports/sampler_comparison.csv,
  reports/prequential.png

  - Solo (Musab): Docker Compose (Mongo + Qdrant + Neo4j + FastAPI) + dataflow
